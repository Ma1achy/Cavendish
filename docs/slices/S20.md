Slice brief. Plan: `plan/REBUILD.md` §9 (row S20), §2 (principle 11), §6. Requirement:
`docs/design/workspace.md` ("the design"; its §0 overrides `docs/design/panels.md`), with

`docs/slices/S19-review-2.md` ("What S20 will have to shrink"; minors 10, 11, 13). Research:
`docs/research/editor-ux.md` (§3.2 layouts, §3.11 windows, §3.13 the camera). No new research

The owner's words set the order: "would rather dense useful gui with good ux over sparse
spacious annoying to use gui"; "a professional scientific app for desktops… everything is
functional and has a purpose, no unnecessary prose".

## 1. What the owner can newly do

- **See more at once.** Rows 20 pt, controls 18 to 20 pt, text 11 to 12 pt, a 4 pt grid; a
  description box as wide as its card; numbers monospace, right-aligned, with their unit; no
  sentences in the chrome (they are tooltips, the `?` sheet and the status bar).
- **Arrange viewports as a mosaic.** Any grid of viewports, written as matplotlib's
  `subplot_mosaic` writes it (`mosaic "AAB;CDB"`), up to twelve: split right or down, close,
  swap two, maximise one (Ctrl+Space), drag the splitters between them (double-click evens them
  out). Each viewport is one view type (Scene, Signal, Periodogram, Flight, Cadence) chosen from
  the first control of its header. A viewport pops out to float over the window and docks back.
- **Read a viewport's header at a glance.** View type, camera menu, shading as four icons,
  an Overlays popover; maximise, pop out and close at the right. No status text in it: the
  instrument, the signal's state, the selection and the scene are in the status bar; a warning
  is one coloured dot whose tooltip says it. A navigation gizmo in each 3-D viewport's top-right
  corner (click an axis to look along it, drag it to orbit; zoom, pan and perspective/orthographic
  under it) replaces the Home button.
- **Pick tools from a palette that floats in the view**, icon-only, dragged by its grip to any
  edge or corner and remembered per layout; Place opens a flyout of what it places. The tool's
  settings are one row under the header, there only while they apply.
- **Run time from a real transport.** Start, step back, play/pause, step forward, end; what
  happens at the end (loop, stop, or run on); speed from 0.1× to 10×; the time and the duration
  typed where they are read (`t 3.20 / 12.00 s`), and the shot.
- **Group things in folders** in the scene tree: new folder, nest, move rows in and out, rename,
  fold; a folder's eye and lock act on everything in it. Folders are saved with the scene.
- **Fly like a first-person game.** Hold the right button (or Shift+F): the pointer is captured,
  the mouse looks, W A S D move along the view, Space rises and Shift sinks, Ctrl or a double tap
  of W sprints, the wheel changes the speed (shown for a moment), movement eases in and out with
  the frame's time. No tool or scope key fires while flying.
- **Walk through the scene.** A physical camera: a player-sized box with gravity, a jump, a step
  up over one voxel, sliding along walls, against the world's voxels, voxel bodies, the floor and
  the room. A double tap of Space switches walking and flying; flying collides too; `noclip` is
  an explicit switch. The camera has no mass and is never in the scene or in what the towers
  read.
- **See a fast circle as a circle.** A body's drawn path follows its motion at any frequency.

Every scene edit above (a folder, the duration) is one `Command` through `Engine::apply`, one
undo. The camera, the mosaic, the palette's place and the transport's speed are not scene edits:
the mosaic and the cameras are view commands (journalled, replayed, never undone); the rest is
the window's own state.

## 2. Design in one page

**Density is the theme's.** `theme::HIT` 24 → 20 (rows, boxes, ticks, drop-downs, eye, lock, ⋮,
`+`); `theme::ICON` 22 (icon buttons: the palette, the header, the transport); `theme::GRID` 4;
text 12 pt (body, buttons), 11 pt (small), 12 pt monospace. Every size a panel uses is read
from the theme, and the density test reads the theme and the laid-out rectangles, not literals.
The 44 pt touch rule is not built here (phones and tablets are S22).

**The mosaic is view state in the engine.** `cav_sim::mosaic::Mosaic`: rows of labels (one
character each, `A`–`Z`), a weight a column and a row. A label's cells must form one rectangle.
A viewport's number is its label's rank in reading order of first appearance, so Tab and the
arrows follow the labels and never which viewport floats. (As built, the number is the label's
alphabetical rank: §7.8.) `ViewCommand::Mosaic` sets it
(journalled, not an edit, as `panes` is); `panes 1|2|4` stay as `A`, `AB`, `AB;CD`.
`MAX_PANES` 4 → 12, refused with a message past it. Split, close and swap are functions from a
mosaic to a mosaic (so they are tested without a window) issued as that one command, the
viewports' cameras and contents carried with their labels. A splitter drag changes two
neighbouring weights, their sum kept, issued once when the drag ends (one journal line a drag).
Maximise and pop-out are the window's state. Pop-out here is a floating frame inside the window
(the design's form for the web, used natively too); OS windows are TASK-S19-02 in S22 (they
cannot be exercised on this PC without a visible window).

**The header is per viewport and made from data.** View types are `PaneContent::ALL`; the
camera menu, shading and overlays are actions of `ui/actions.ron` run on that viewport. The
status bar gains what the header loses, drawn from the same engine answers (bit for bit).

**The palette** is an `egui::Area` inside the active scene viewport's canvas, placed by an
anchor (one of eight edges and corners) kept per layout name in the window's state. It never
changes the canvas's size.

**Transport.** Buttons are actions of `ui/actions.ron` (`seek start`, `step -1`, `play`, `step 1`,
`seek end`, `end loop|stop|run-on`, `speed <x>`). A step lands on the engine's own shot times
(`Engine::shots`), never worked out in the window. *Run on*: at the end the scene's duration
grows by its length at the start of the run (a `Set` of `duration`, each extension continuing
the same edit: one undo for a whole run), to the schema's limit, where it stops and says so. A
live mode with a rolling window, bounded in memory, is not built here (TASK-S22-01).

**Folders are scene data.** `SceneSpec.folders` (label, group, parent) and an optional `folder`
on a body and a tower, none of them written when empty, so every existing scene file and spec
hash is unchanged to the byte. `folder new|move|rename|remove` are edits. A folder's eye and
lock apply the existing eye (`hide`) and lock to what it holds.

**The first-person camera** is arithmetic on the view (`cav_app::camera`), integrated a frame
at a time with the frame's dt: a velocity that eases toward the wanted one (time constant
0.08 s), yaw and pitch from raw pointer motion, pitch held within ±89°, no roll. Pointer
capture is the platform's (`CursorGrab` natively, pointer lock in a page).

**The walker** (`cav_app::walk`) is a box (0.6 × 0.6 × 1.8 m, eye at 1.62 m) moved by swept
axis-by-axis tests against an `Occupancy` (is this box free?), made of the world's voxels, the
voxel bodies' trees at the playhead, the floor, the room's shell, and, where the scene has
nothing below, its ground level. It reads the scene and writes only the camera.

**A drawn path is sampled by its curvature, not by the clock.** The path is cut where the
motion breaks (appears, vanishes) and each piece is subdivided until the chord's sag is under
a tolerance, within a cap of segments; a periodic motion is drawn over one period. *Bound:* the
drawn polyline lies within 1 % of the path's local radius of curvature (or 1 cm) of the true
path, or the cap is reached and the path is flagged decimated.

## 6. Performance budget

Recorded, not gated (the owner's ruling of 21 Nov 2026), release on the build PC: a frame of
the window's own drawing with six viewports and no GPU surface ≤ 4 ms; a walker step ≤ 50 µs
against `town`; a path of 10⁴ periods drawn in ≤ 2 ms within the segment cap; `quick` stays
under 30 s warm (slow tests take `slow_` into their names).

### 7.8 Not built, or built differently

- **A viewport's number is its label's alphabetical rank**, not its rank in reading order of
  first appearance as §2 says: M5 (the order survives a swap of positions) needs numbers that a
  swap does not change.
- **Pop-out is a floating frame inside the window.** OS windows are TASK-S19-02 in S22.
- **Swapping two viewports by dragging a header** is implemented and has no test by events.
- **Folders**: none in Surroundings; a folder cannot be selected to move or delete what it holds
  (it needs multi-selection, S22); Delete on a lit folder does nothing.
- **The camera**: orbit and pan from the keyboard are not eased (in `plan/IDEAS.md`).
- **The transport**: "as fast as it runs" is left out: playback moves a playhead over a signal
  already computed, so there is nothing to run faster. The live rolling mode is TASK-S22-01.
- **The palette** is drawn in its viewport's layer, not as an `egui::Area` (§2 said an Area): it
  clips with the canvas and never floats over a neighbour.
- **The header** has no bookmarks, follow, log/linear, export, grid or labels toggles: no command
  exists for them yet. A plot's header has no series picker.
- **The pointer lock in a page** compiles and is untried in a real browser (this PC's rule: no
  browsers); the native capture is tested by the commands the window is asked for (C4).
- **A body in several modes at once** (`Modes`) still has no drawn path.
- **Layouts made with `+`** last the session; they are not saved with the scene.
- The 44 pt touch rule, docking with tabs, Scale, Rotate, Measure, Slice, Paint, multi-selection,
  units typed with values, bookmarks, the browser's reserved keys, a user keymap, phones and
  tablets: S22, as §3 says. Their rows in `docs/slices/S19.md` §4 stand unmeasured.

### 7.9 Decided (worth a second look)

- Density: the row gap is 2 pt (pitch 22, off the 4 pt grid); the inspector is 320 pt, not 300
  (X, Y, Z boxes need six figures); the label column is a third; number boxes are 2 pt short of
  the design's; an optional number's × stays beside its tick.
- Mosaic: a split halves the weight of what it splits; close gives the space to the left, above,
  right, below, in that order; Home's undo step stores a pane number, so after a close renumbers
  the viewports an earlier Home's undo lands on whichever has that number (not fixed; in IDEAS);
  the digest and `stats` include the mosaic.
- Header: its keyboard scope holds the active viewport's eight controls, a Rust constant (the
  menus' rows are from `actions.ron`); maximise is disabled on a floating viewport; a decimation
  note lights the dot on every scene viewport; a status part over 32 characters is cut (whole in
  its tooltip).
- Transport: a step counts scheduled shots that were not taken too; typing a duration during a
  run on restarts the run's block; a run on that finds the last extension's signal still stale
  adds twice as many blocks.
- Folders: a click on a folder's row issues `select none`; a new folder and its name are two
  undo steps; `set bodies[n].folder` by path fails while the field is absent.
- Camera: flying collides by default; starting inside matter lifts the box straight up; the
  world's collision is analytic from the spec's pieces (terrain from the column's middle sample:
  it can differ from the drawn cells by one cell at supersample ≥ 2); the room has no door
  (noclip to enter or leave); a net at the ground level under everything; a session holds the
  target 1 m ahead with the near plane at 2 cm and restores the orbit distance, and an
  orthographic view becomes perspective at the first move; default fly speed 4 m/s (was 2); in
  a page Ctrl+W closes the tab, so sprint there is the double tap (for TASK-S19-11).
- Here: a popped-out viewport opens inside the figure; a screenshot is retaken while the world
  streams; no validation case was added (§7.6).
