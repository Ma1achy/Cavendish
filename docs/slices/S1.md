## 1. What the owner can newly do

- **Put a mass in the scene and drag it**: `mass 10 at 3 0 1` in the console (or load `point`), select
  it, drag it in a pane. Every tower's phase is read again as it moves; the readings are beside the
  towers in the outliner, on the figure and in the properties.
- **Play a straight pass** (`load pass`, Space): the mass moves, the playhead runs along the lanes, and
  the signal plot draws each tower's series with the playhead on it.
- **Plot the signal** in any pane: `show signal`. **Plot its periodogram**: `show periodogram`, with
  the comb of each oscillating body drawn over it: a stem at every harmonic `n·f₀`, folded about the
  Nyquist frequency where it lies above it, and labelled as folded.
- **Edit anything by path**: `set bodies[0].mass 20`, `set array.instrument.launch_floor false`; undo
  takes it back. `signal`, `reading` and `periodogram` answer in the console, `cav exec` and MCP's
  `command` tool.
- **Load the new dev scenes**: `point`, `pass`, `oscillator-zoo`, `superposition`, `mirror-pair`,
  `switch-on`, `flash`, `time-reversal`, `ring-orbit`, `through-the-tower`, `precision-far`.
- **Run the oracle tier**: `cargo xtask oracle` compiles v1 at `v1-final` and holds the engine to it and
  to George's scripts. `cargo xtask validate --report --against s0-shell` is the slice-end check.

## 2. Design in one page

### The forward model (`cav-core::forward`, f64)

For interferometer `k` of a tower, `δφ_k = (m_A/ħ) ∫₀^2T [V(upper arm) − V(lower arm)] dt`; the
gradiometer reads `ΔΦ = δφ₁ − δφ₂`.

| Choice | The engine's default | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Sign | lower minus upper, `δφ₁ − δφ₂` | George, 21 Nov 2026 |
| Which is interferometer 1 | **the lower one** | confirmed in v1's code (`build_arms` returns `[make(0.0), make(cfg.ifo_sep)]`, and `delta_phi` is `d2 − d1`: upper minus lower) and in the scripts (`lower1`/`upper1` launch from z = 0, `lower2`/`upper2` from z = δr; the series is `δφ₁ − δφ₂`) |
| Timing | atom and source at the same instant | George |
| Atom | ⁸⁷Sr, 86.908 877 5 u × 1.660 539 068 92 × 10⁻²⁷ kg = 1.443 155 87 × 10⁻²⁵ kg | NIST Atomic Weights and Isotopic Compositions; CODATA 2022 (both read 21 Nov 2026; `cav-core::constants`) |
| ħ, G | 1.054 571 817 × 10⁻³⁴ J s (exact SI); 6.674 30 × 10⁻¹¹ | SI 2019; CODATA 2022 |
| Launch | u₀ = 3.86 m/s (AION-10) | George |
| Floor | an arm that falls back to its launch height stops there with no velocity; before T the kick starts it again from rest; after T it waits for the next sequence | George: "coded as the Python scripts code the sequence" (`atom_path`: `if z <= floor and v < 0: z = floor; v = 0; break`) |
| Kick | `v_kick = 1000 ħ k / m_A`, λ = 698 nm: 6.578 m/s with the constants above (6.505 with the references' rounded ones) | scripts and v1 |
| g | 9.81 m/s² | scripts and v1 |

Floor, mass, ħ, G and sign are fields of `Instrument`; the quadrature is a field too
(`Quadrature::{Gauss, Simpson, ScriptTrapezoid}`), and all of them feed **one** integrator: a list of
nodes (source time, weight, the two arms' rise) summed against a `Source`. v1's conventions and the
scripts' are instruments, not code paths: `Instrument::with_v1_conventions()` (no floor, v1's mass and
ħ, upper minus lower, Simpson at 0.01 s on each half) and `Quadrature::ScriptTrapezoid { lag }` (the
scripts' nodes; `lag = 1` as published, `lag = 0` with the offset corrected).

**The mass enters almost only through the trajectories.** `(m_A/ħ)·v_kick = 1000·k` whatever the mass,
so in a uniform gradient the phase does not depend on `m_A` at all; PROGRESS.md's "1.2%" is the change
in the mass, not in the phase. What changes is the arms' reach (the kick is 1.1% larger) and where
they land: with ⁸⁷Sr the AION-10 arms would close 1.8 cm below the launch height at 2T, not 7.2 cm.

**The engine's quadrature** is Gauss–Legendre, six nodes a panel, panels of at most 0.1 s, on pieces
cut at every kink: the π pulse, each arm's landing, and every time at which the source is not smooth
(a body appears or vanishes; a pass starts or stops). v1's Simpson rule runs straight through kinks
and is 1e-8 to 3e-7 from the converged integral (research note §3); it is kept only as the rule that
ties the engine to v1.

**Precision rules of §3.4, on the CPU now:** a source's position is turned into an offset from the
tower's base in f64 before anything else; arm heights are the rise above the arm's own launch point;
`1/|a| − 1/|b|` is formed as `(|b|² − |a|²)/(|a||b|(|a| + |b|))` with `|b|² − |a|²` from the arms'
separation; the flight's sum is in kg s/m and `G·m_A/ħ` multiplies it once.

**Sources in S1:** a point mass or uniform sphere on a path (`Mass`); an infinite uniform slab below
the array (`Slab`); a finite disc on a tower's axis (`AxialDisc`, for the edge-term convergence only).
A sphere is a point outside itself and has the closed-form interior potential, so it may cross an
atom's path where a point may not.

### Analysis (`cav-core::spectrum`, f64 on the CPU)

The classical periodogram `S_k = (Δt/N)|FFT(y − ȳ)|²` at `f_k = k/(NΔt)`, one-sided, not doubled,
with the `1D_oscillator` variant (no mean subtraction, DC kept); the exact Lomb–Scargle of the
scripts (Scargle's τ, pre-centred, times `dt_eff`); `fold_frequency`; a least-squares notch; and, if
it fits, the NUFFT Lomb–Scargle (§6 says whether it did). No GPU periodogram (owner's ruling).

### The oracle crate (`oracle/`, outside the workspace)

v1's `instrument`, `gravity` and `source` at tag `v1-final` as a git dependency, compiled only by
`cargo xtask oracle` and `cargo xtask validate`; George's scripts as fixtures
(`oracle/fixtures/scripts/`, made by `scripts/reference_fixtures.py`, provenance beside them).

### 3.1 Criteria written before measuring

- **R7, 1e-10:** v1's rule is reproduced node for node, so what is left is rounding in two different
  orders of the same arithmetic: expected about 1e-13 of peak.
- **R9, 1e-9:** the research note's independent re-implementation got 2e-13 and 5e-16.
- **R10, 3e-4 of peak:** the corrected scripts still use the trapezoid rule at 0.01 s (the research
  note measured 4e-5 to 1e-4 of peak for that alone) and, in the `fig2_*` and `100m` scripts, stop one
  step short of 2T (estimated 1e-4 for AION-10, 1e-5 for AICE). The engine is run with the scripts'
  constants, so nothing else differs. If a case measures above 3e-4 the cause is found and written
  here; the number is not moved.
- **R11, 1e-11:** against the same rule with twice the nodes a panel and panels a quarter as long.
- **R13:** above a disc of radius R the leading edge term is a uniform gradient `2πGρh/R`, so the
  gradiometer reads `Δr/R` of what one interferometer reads from the slab: 7.8e-4 at R = 6.4 km.
- **R14, 1e-11:** an offset formed from coordinates of 1e4 m is good to 2e-12 m, 4e-13 of 5 m.
- **R21:** each arm is its own mirror image about T only when both arms close at the launch height
  at 2T, which is the AICE launch rule; AION-10's 3.86 m/s with the floor is not symmetric.

## 6. Where the plan, the references and this slice disagree

Filled in as the slice runs; the results and the departures are in §7.

- `reference/design/instrument.md` §4 has `ΔΦ = δφ₂ − δφ₁`, ⁸⁸Sr's mass under ⁸⁷Sr's name, and "a
  reflecting floor" which v1 never coded. George's answers replace all three; the plan wins.
- `plan/REBUILD.md` §3.4 says "the read is upper-minus-lower": v1's convention. The engine's is
  George's.
- `harmonic_heights_fall_as_amplitude_ratio` is stated in the plan as `(A/√X)ⁿ` with `X` "the squared
  distance scale of the paper". Without the paper `X` cannot be checked. What is tested is the part
  that does not need it: the n-th harmonic's height relative to the fundamental grows as `A^(n−1)`.
- "Forced motion on a sphere" (report §2.5.1) is not in the scripts read. The `Sphere` motion here is
  this slice's own parametrisation and is marked so; it is a question for George.
